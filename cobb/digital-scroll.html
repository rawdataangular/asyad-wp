Transshipment and ICD Confirmation
The Transition from an Inland Container Depot (ICD) to the Port of Loading (POL), and finally through a Transshipment (T/S) hub, is where the most expensive mistakes happen. If the "handshake" between these points isn't perfect, you face missed vessels, massive storage costs, and vessel stowage chaos.
A container's journey is a relay race where the hand-off is everything. inTEUtion digitizes the critical confirmation process between ICDs, POLs, and Transshipment hubs. Our platform ensures that before a rail or truck even departs the inland depot, the destination port and T/S agent have provided a "Go-Ahead" based on real-time vessel schedules. No more deviations, no more "blind" shipments—just perfectly timed arrivals that align with terminal stowage plans and berth allotments.
